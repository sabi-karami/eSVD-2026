eSVD-2026

An updated, evidence-based extension of the classic Total Cerebral Small Vessel Disease (SVD) Score (Staals et al., 2014), adding cortical superficial siderosis and cortical atrophy for a fuller 0–6 picture of small vessel disease burden on MRI.

⚠️ Research & educational use only. This calculator is not a certified medical device and must not replace the judgement of a qualified neuroradiologist or neurologist. Always correlate with the full clinical and imaging context.

MRI Findings

Vascular markers
White matter hyperintensities (Fazekas)

A point is scored if periventricular grade = 3 or deep grade ≥ 2 (Staals 2014 definition).

Enlarged perivascular spaces (EPVS)

A point is scored at grade ≥ 2 (≥ 11 EPVS in the basal ganglia).

eSVD-2026 extension markers NEW

A point is scored at GCA ≥ 2 (moderate–severe cortical atrophy).
